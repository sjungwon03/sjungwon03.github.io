I'm Jungwon Sohn, a cloud infrastructure and backend engineer.

For two years and three months I owned the backend of a government-funded vocational training LMS at Smart Dong School. Course progress in that service was the basis for tuition reimbursement, and instructor settlement figures fed actual payroll — so before asking whether a feature worked, I had to ask whether the numbers were right. That framing stuck.

Most of what I did there was change things that couldn't stop running. Moving four years of Express onto NestJS while a live mobile API depended on it. Consolidating eleven forked PHP sites so the same fix didn't have to be made eleven times. Pulling lecture tracking out of the monolith because every deploy was destroying learners' progress.

I handled deployment and operations alongside development, and moved into infrastructure from there. I built CI/CD across five repositories, implemented blue/green on a single instance, and codified infrastructure that had only ever existed as console clicks. After leaving I earned the AWS Solutions Architect – Associate and Terraform Associate certifications, and led infrastructure on a hybrid Proxmox and AWS build across four physical servers.

These days I build in the open — MCP servers, AI agents, developer tools, cloud-native services — and ship two mobile apps solo.

## What goes here

Notes on things I built and the reasoning behind them, including the parts that didn't work. I try to write down the alternatives I rejected and why, because that's usually the part that's missing when I read someone else's write-up.
